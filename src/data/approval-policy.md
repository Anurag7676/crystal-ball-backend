## [policy-1] Scope and roles

This approval policy covers content submitted to the digital-twin workspace: folders, videos, PDFs and images. HelpAdmins submit content; operators review it. An operator must not review content they submitted themselves. Every item enters the queue with the status "Pending Review" and stays there until an operator records a decision. The assistant can explain and summarise the queue, but it cannot approve or reject anything.

## [policy-2] Review workflow

Step 1: Open the item and confirm the title, type and submitter match the queue entry. Step 2: Check the content is complete — folders contain every listed checklist, videos play end to end, PDFs open and are legible, images are full resolution. Step 3: Check accuracy against the site it describes, such as zone boundaries, camera positions and equipment models. Step 4: Record a decision of Approve or Reject. A rejection must include a written reason so the submitter can fix and resubmit.

## [policy-3] Safety-related content

Content describing safety equipment, sensors, emergency procedures or restricted zones is safety-related. Safety-related items should be reviewed before other pending items, and the reviewer must confirm that equipment specifications match the models currently deployed on site. If the reviewer is unsure, they should leave the item pending and escalate to the site safety lead rather than approving it.

## [policy-4] Media quality and resubmission

Videos are required to be at least 720p resolution, with legible on-screen text. Images used as maps or layouts must show a scale or a reference marker. Rejected items return to the submitter, who can resubmit a corrected version; a resubmission re-enters the queue as a new pending item and is reviewed from Step 1. Items without a decision for more than 5 working days should be flagged to the operations manager.
